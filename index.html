A Blessing for My Life 🙏

“For I know the plans I have for you,” declares the LORD, “plans to prosper you and not to harm you, plans to give you hope and a future.”

Jeremiah 29:11 — NIV

Heavenly Father, thank You for the precious gift of life and for carrying me through every season. Thank You for the visible blessings and the quiet victories that only You and I know about.

As I begin another year, give me wisdom when I must make difficult decisions, courage when I feel afraid, strength when life becomes heavy, and faith when I cannot see what lies ahead. Bless my family, protect the people I love, and help me become a blessing to others.

A note from my heart

A New Chapter, A Grateful Heart ❤️

Today is more than just my birthday. It is a reminder of how far God has brought me, the battles He has helped me overcome, the silent prayers He has heard, and the blessings that have kept me going even when the journey was not easy.

Life has taught me that growing does not mean having everything figured out. Sometimes it means standing up after disappointment, learning from mistakes, and finding the courage to try again. I am still growing, still learning, still dreaming and still trusting God with my future.

To everyone who has been part of my story, through a prayer, a kind word, a phone call, a visit or encouragement, thank you. You may never fully know how much your presence has meant to me.

As I step into this new chapter, I pray for wisdom, courage, strength, good health for my loved ones, and a heart that never forgets where it came from.

May this year bring growth where there has been struggle, peace where there has been worry, light where there has been uncertainty, and beautiful opportunities where there have been closed doors.

Happy birthday to me. 🎂❤️ The journey continues, and by God's grace, I believe the best chapters are still ahead.

With a grateful heart,
Levi 💛
